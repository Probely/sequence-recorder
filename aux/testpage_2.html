Toggles, labels and page-script clicks

Start recording, go through the cards (reload the page between groups if you like), then stop and compare the recording with each card's Expect. The green status line shows checkbox states and handler counts: after replaying the recording on a fresh load, it should show what it showed at the end of recording. Orange cards document behavior that is accepted or not handled.
